I am excited to dive into the Office Superfan Season 7 episodes and finish the rest of Foundation. Mini-LED or OLED would be cool, plus ProMotion is a must in the next generation. </p><p>Even though I am not planning on using or characterizing the iPad Mini as a productivity device, I have been thinking more and more about setting up a Personal Knowledge Management system for myself, and the iPad Mini would be a great place to possibly add to it but mostly access it.</p><h3 id="returning-to-the-mac">Returning to the Mac</h3><figure class="kg-card kg-image-card"><img src="/content/images/2024/01/DSC_0576.jpg" class="kg-image" alt="" loading="lazy" width="2000" height="1200"></figure><p>The iPad Pro-Only life has been fun. The 2021 iPad Pro 11-inch with the M1 chip is more than I will ever need in a computer when it comes to performance. For writing, personal admin tasks, photo editing, or light front-end development it is more than capable. </p><p>I am also a huge fan of iPadOS and Stage Manager, especially with an external monitor. The iPad has come a long way and I am a big advocate of an iPad being more than enough for most people. Some have a very critical take when it comes to the iPad and if it doesn't meet certain specific standards it gets a bad wrap and is seen as a bad computer.</p><p>Even though I think the iPad Pro is capable, I think a more honest approach when it comes to choosing it over another platform is personal preference. I like using both macOS and iPadOS, and both provide me with a very different but good experience. macOS can feel dated sometimes while iPadOS can feel new and futuristic. </p><p>At the same time, iPadOS can sometimes feel constricting whereas macOS feels very open. Notice how I have not said anything negative or positive about the difference because this isn't about what is good or bad, but what is different between the two.</p><p>I could say both of these differences in a different way: macOS is more established and robust compared to the newer still refining iPadOS, or iPadOS allows for more focus and direction whereas macOS can provide too broad of choices and uncontrollable. </p><p>All this is to say that I am considering going back to a Mac as my main computer. Not because I like it more than iPadOS in general, but for what I need right now it feels like my preferred tool. I am working on building a PKM, automation for my blog/newsletter, and my job has tasked me with some new development opportunities and macOS has some tools I would prefer to use as I develop my skills. </p><p>I haven't made the transition yet, but looking at my options in where I want to go next. The MacBook Pro in Space Black is very tempting, but a Midnight MacBook Air has always caught my eye.
